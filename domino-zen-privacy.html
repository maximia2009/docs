Domino Zen Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026 · Version 2.0

Domino Zen is developed by Li Shichao. You can build and play without an account. Signing in is only needed to publish, like or report works in the Plaza, or to buy Pro.

1. On Your Device

Your saved works, settings and the cached Plaza copies stay on your device. They are not uploaded unless you publish a work.

2. If You Sign In with Apple

  • The account identifier Apple gives us, and the email address if you choose to share it (it may be an Apple private relay address).
  • The display name you set, shown on works you publish.
  • An Apple sign-in token, stored encrypted, used only to revoke the sign-in when you delete your account.

3. Plaza

  • Works you publish: title, tags, the domino layout and a small preview image. Published works are public.
  • Likes you give, reports you file and authors you block.
  • Moderation records when a work is hidden, removed or restored.

4. Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We store the App Store transaction identifiers and subscription status needed to unlock Pro. We never see your payment details.

5. What We Don't Do

  • No advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library access.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties for their own use.

6. Where Data Is Stored

Account and Plaza data is hosted by Supabase in the United States (us-east-1).

7. Deleting Your Account

Open Account in the app and choose Delete Account. We remove your account, sign-in tokens, likes and reports, and revoke the Apple sign-in. Works you published may stay in the Plaza without your name. Cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

8. Children

The Plaza is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data.
