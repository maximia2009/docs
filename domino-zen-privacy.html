Domino Zen Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026 · Version 1.0

Domino Zen is developed by Li Shichao as a mindful 3D domino physics sandbox. We deeply respect your privacy: Domino Zen does not collect, track, or share any personal data.

1. Zero Data Collection

We believe your creative time should be peaceful and private. Domino Zen:

  • Does not require registration, accounts, or sign-ins.
  • Does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts.
  • Does not access location data, camera, microphone, or photo library.
  • Does not track device identifiers, advertising IDs (IDFA), or IP addresses.

2. Local Storage Only

All domino builds, custom arrangements, stage colors, camera configurations, and saved works are stored exclusively on your device using local sandboxed file storage. Your designs never leave your hardware.

3. No Third-Party SDKs or Analytics

Domino Zen contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, tracking libraries, or analytics frameworks. The application makes no background network requests to third-party tracking servers.

4. In-App Purchases

Domino Zen is free to download. If optional digital content or expansion themes are made available in the future, payments will be processed directly through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Domino Zen does not process or retain your payment card details.

5. Children’s Privacy

Domino Zen is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, our app fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.
