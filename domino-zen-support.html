Welcome to Domino Zen support. Here you can find tips for building, camera controls, troubleshooting, and direct developer contact.

How to Build & Play

  • Draw Paths: Drag your finger anywhere across the stage to draw continuous lines of dominoes. As you reach screen edges, the canvas gracefully expands.
  • Place Single Pieces: Tap on empty ground to position individual tiles. Enable Precision Mode in Settings to place tiles without accidentally toppling standing ones.
  • Start Chain Reactions: Gently tap any upright domino to topple it and set the cascade in motion.
  • Camera Controls: Drag with one or two fingers to orbit and inspect your creation. Pinch to zoom in or out.
  • Undo / Reset: Use the Undo button in the navigation bar or press ⌘Z on an external keyboard to undo actions. Tap Reset to restore all fallen tiles upright.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my works backed up to iCloud?

Works are stored locally on your device for immediate, zero-latency access and privacy.

Can I adjust graphics performance?

Yes. Open Settings (gear icon) to toggle dynamic resolution, soft shadows, and ambient occlusion (SSAO) to tailor visual fidelity and battery life.
